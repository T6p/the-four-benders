# Stances (sub-bending)

A stance is a deeper art of your element. Taking one changes what your keys do while you are in it. Each stance's moves are on its element's page: [lava and metal](earth.md), [plants, blood and healing](water.md), [lightning and combustion](fire.md), [the spirit](air.md).

| Element | Stances |
|---|---|
| Earth | lava, metal (sand comes with plain earth) |
| Water | plants, blood, healing |
| Fire | lightning, combustion |
| Air | spirit |

## Taking a stance
Hold **Left Alt** for the stance wheel, point at a stance and let go. Tap **Left Alt** to go back to your plain element. The wheel only shows stances you have opened.

## How each stance opens
Some stances open from a scroll, some from where you bend (a hidden **affinity**, see below), some from practice.

| Stance | How it opens |
|---|---|
| **Lava** | grow the lava affinity by bending earth in heat, by lava; or read Ghazan's *Lava Flood* scroll |
| **Metal** | the badgermoles' style (see [Progression](progression.md#the-four-teachers)); or grow the metal affinity by bending earth deep underground near ore |
| **Plants** | grow the plants affinity by bending water among leaves and roots, with little open water |
| **Blood** | read Hama's scroll; or grow the blood affinity (water by night, little water near, living creatures near). The grown route also needs water mastery of 0.8 (Expert or better) |
| **Healing** | opens once your water has healed anything |
| **Lightning** | grow the lightning affinity by firebending in thunderstorms under the open sky; or read the *Redirection* scroll |
| **Combustion** | opens once you have practised it, or at fire mastery 0.75 |
| **Spirit** | opens once you have practised it, or at air mastery 0.75 |

## Affinities
Affinities are hidden. Every bend that gives you practice also looks at where you are: the blocks, creatures, sky, weather and biome. Bending in a fitting place grows the matching affinity. Nothing shows the number, but a line of words comes at a third and at two thirds of the way. A full affinity opens its art once, with a chat card and a burst.

| Affinity | Element | Grows when you bend | Opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| plants | water | among leaves and roots, little open water | plants stance |
| blood | water | by night, little water near, creatures near | blood stance (waits for water mastery 0.8) |
| ice | water | in snow and ice | an ice art |
| metal | earth | deep underground near ore | metal stance |
| lava | earth | in heat, by lava | lava stance |
| sand | earth | in deserts and on sand | a sand art |
| lightning | fire | in thunderstorms under the open sky | lightning stance |
| breath | fire | in cold and rain | Breath of Fire |
| flight | air | high up in the open sky | a flight art |
| being | air | among creatures | the vacuum (waits for air mastery 0.75) |

Practice made where an affinity fits also feeds its stance, so a waterbender who lives in a jungle grows their plants as they bend water there.

## Growing a stance
Stances grow like elements, from using them, but **far slower**, and only when you use every move of the stance. One move alone tops out early (one blood move alone stops at about 0.39). A new stance is weak (about 0.4 of full strength) and reaches 1.15 at full.

Early stages:
- **Lava**: at first the ground only smokes; later it turns to magma at most; then real lava.
- **Metal**: when new, your hold on metal fails often (60% of the time).
- **Lightning and combustion**: when very new, they may fizzle.
