## Bender NPCs
There are four kinds: earth-, water-, fire- and airbenders. They **don't spawn naturally**: you meet them in structures, from spawn eggs (Spawn Eggs tab), or from `/avatar npc`. They never despawn when far away; Peaceful only removes the wild ones.

| Rank | Health | Armour | Chi | Reacts to an attack | Aim | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Novice | 20 | 0 | 50 | 25% of the time | rough | stands still, long pauses |
| Adept | 30 | 2 | 80 | 60% | good | circles you |
| Master | 50 | 6 | 260 | 95% | near perfect | circles fast, switches sides, leads a running target |

- They keep their own distance, spot incoming fire, water, gusts, thrown rock and projectiles within 9 blocks, and shield, dodge or wall. They have no cooldowns, only a chi pool.
- Masters have signature moves: a firebender splits your fire as it reaches them and punches the ground into a fire bomb; an airbender raises a tornado round itself that throws back what you throw; a waterbender flings razor rings and closes an ice dome over itself when hurt; an earthbender sends rock slides and closes the earth over itself when hurt.
- A bender who is hit calls nearby benders of the same element to help. Benders of different elements fight each other.
- **Residents and teachers** leave you alone unless attacked.
- **Village customs:** every bender of one element in one area shares a hidden fighting custom (quick, heavy, guarded, close or far). The game never names it; you notice it.
- Their bending stops in their element's eclipse, when chi-blocked, when bloodbent, or when the Avatar takes it (they become a "Former …" bender).
- **Drops:** a technique scroll of their element (novice 30%, adept 50%, master 90%).
- **Teachers:** right-click a master with an empty hand. Your own element: they show you a form. Your next element on the Avatar cycle: a spar. See [Progression](progression.md#masters-and-forms).

## Creatures
**Sky bison.** 80 health, no fall damage. Wild ones live on high peaks, snowy slopes, meadows and groves; two are placed at every Air Temple.
- Feed it hay (50% chance to tame each time), an apple or melon slice (30%), or wheat (20%). Hay heals the most.
- Use a saddle on your bison: four ride it with a saddle, two bareback. Only the owner steers: look and move forward to fly, **Jump** to climb or take off, let go and it hangs in the air, **Shift** to climb off.
- The **bison whistle** calls your nearest tamed bison within 160 blocks (from anywhere once bonded).
- Airbenders can bond with it (see [the four teachers](progression.md#the-four-teachers)).

**Badgermoles.** 70 health, armour 6, hit hard (9). Blind; they only fight back when hit. They live in badgermole dens. Music (a goat horn, a note block, or a jukebox within 10 blocks) calms one for 90 seconds; while calm it tunnels through earth, and the tunnel closes after 20 seconds. Hitting it ends the calm.

**Ran and Shaw.** The last dragons, red and blue, at the Sun Warrior ruins. They can't be harmed; hitting one makes it breathe fire on you. The dance:
1. Right-click the **Eternal Flame** with an empty hand to take a Sun Warriors' Flame.
2. Right-click the **Sun Stone** with the flame. Resting dragons within 48 blocks wake.
3. Follow their dance for 8 seconds. Slip, or go more than 14 blocks from the stone, and you are found unworthy and burned.

Everyone found worthy earns "Found Worthy". A firebender also gets the dragons' fire.

**Tui and La.** The spirit koi circle the spirit pool in the Water Tribe village and can't be harmed. Waterbenders learn the moon's blessing there.

**Past Avatars.** A spirit at an Avatar shrine's altar; once per game day, for 16 seconds.

## Structures

| Structure | Where | Inside |
|---|---|---|
| **Air Temple** | jagged, frozen and stony peaks, snowy slopes, groves | air master (teacher), resident adepts and a novice, 2 sky bison, chests |
| **Water Tribe village** | snowy plains, ice spikes, snowy taiga, snowy beach | water master (teacher), residents, the spirit pool with Tui and La, chests |
| **Earth Kingdom fort** | plains, sunflower plains, meadow, savanna | earth master (teacher), residents, chests |
| **Fire Nation temple** | badlands, desert | fire master (teacher), residents, chests |
| **Avatar shrine** | plains, forests, flower and birch forests, meadow, taiga, cherry grove | the Avatar Altar, a chest with one scroll of each element |
| **Sun Warrior ruins** | jungles | Eternal Flame, Sun Stone, Ran and Shaw, a chest |
| **Badgermole den** | windswept and gravelly hills, forest, stony peaks, meadow | badgermoles, a chest that always has a goat horn |
| **Fire camp** | savanna, plains, desert, badlands | **hostile** fire adept and novices, a chest |
| **Earth outpost** | plains, meadow, forest, windswept hills, taiga | earth adept and novice residents, a chest |
| **Ice huts** | snowy plains, taiga and beach, grove, ice spikes | a water adept resident, a chest |
| **Air ruin** | windswept hills, stony peaks, meadow, grove, snowy slopes | a chest |

The four nation homes are the rarest (one every ~44-48 chunks); camps, huts and ruins are common.

**Chest loot:**
- **Nation homes:** 1-2 technique scrolls of the element plus extras. Air: glider staff, wool, candles, feathers. Water: water skin, fish, packed ice. Earth: cable spool leggings, iron, emeralds. Fire: flint and steel, coal, blaze powder.
- **Shrine:** one scroll of each element, a golden apple, candles, amethyst and XP bottles.
- **Camps:** one scroll of any element and supplies.
- **Sun Warrior ruins:** fire scrolls, gold, a golden apple, fire charges.
- **Badgermole den:** a goat horn, a note block and the music disc "cat", plus supplies.
- Technique scrolls also turn up in 15 kinds of vanilla chest (dungeons, mineshafts, temples, strongholds, villages and more), 30% each. Teaching scrolls are rarer: see [Progression > Scrolls](progression.md#scrolls).

## Blocks
- **Avatar Altar**: right-click and stay near it for 3 seconds to wake the Avatar State. Unbreakable.
- **Eternal Flame** and **Sun Stone**: the dragon dance blocks. Unbreakable.
- **Frozen Water** and **Ice**: made by waterbending; fragile, drop nothing, and melt after a while.
- **Vine Pillar**: a temporary block from the plants stance.

## The sky
The sun and moon change how strong fire and water are.

| When | Effect |
|---|---|
| Day | fire ×1.2 |
| Night | fire ×0.85 (masters lose less), water ×1.2 |
| Full moon | water ×1.8 |
| **The comet** | fire ×2.5 as strong and ×1.5 as big. Comes every 30 days, stays a day, warned a day before |
| **Solar eclipse** | no firebending at all for 5 minutes, for players and NPCs. Every 20 days, around midday |
| **Lunar eclipse** | no waterbending at all for 5 minutes. Every 25 days, at night (the moon turns red) |

Each event's next date is counted from the end of the last one. Operators can start or stop them with `/avatar comet` and `/avatar eclipse`.

## Advancements
The mod's advancement tab, **The Four Elements**, follows your path:
- **The Element Answers** (bend for the first time), then **Master of the Tides / Stone / Flame / Winds** (reach Master), **Arrows of a Master** (airbending tattoos), **In Perfect Balance** (master all four).
- **A Deeper Art** (take up a stance) and **All the Deeper Arts** (all eight).
- **Where Bending Began**: find one of the first benders' teachers. **Riding the Wind** (ride a sky bison high), **Ear to the Ground** (walk beside a calm badgermole as it digs), **The Last Dragons** (wake Ran and Shaw at the Sun Stone), **Under the Moon** (keep still by the spirit pool at night).
- The teachers' lessons: **A Gentle Giant** (tame a bison), **A Friend for Life** (bison bond), **Seeing Through Stone** (badgermole sense), **Found Worthy** and **Colours of the Flame** (the dragons), **Blessed by the Moon** and **Back from the Brink** (saved by Spirit Water).
- **Pressure Points** and **Stop the Flow** (chi-blocking).
- **The Next Element** (taught by a master after a spar), **Four as One** (become the Avatar), **The Glow Within** (wake the State at an altar), **Every Life at Once** (enter the Avatar State).
- **Voices of the Past** (meet a past Avatar), and a few hidden ones about energybending.
