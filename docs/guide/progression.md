# Progression

How you grow from a newborn bender to the Avatar. Numbers are the default server config; a server can change them.

**On this page:** [Chi](#chi) · [Mastery](#mastery) · [Training](#training) · [The four teachers](#the-four-teachers) · [Masters and forms](#masters-and-forms) · [Scrolls](#scrolls) · [The Avatar cycle](#the-avatar-cycle)

## Chi
Chi is what bending costs. Holding and moving matter, throws, blocks torn out of the ground and stomps all spend it.

- Base chi is **100**, multiplied by your mastery: from about 240 as a novice to 480 as a master. **Earthbenders get twice the chi and refill twice as fast.**
- Newborns start with a quarter of that pool. It grows to the full pool as your six keys grow.
- Chi refills at 12 per second, but only a quarter as fast while you are bending or spent chi in the last second. Mastery speeds refilling too.
- In the spirit stance, holding **R** still to meditate refills chi three times faster.
- Some fixed costs: punching earth out costs 7 extra, a flip 6, a dodge (**Z**) 10, a sweep (**Shift + Z**) 14.
- **At zero chi** you drop whatever you hold and are **exhausted**: you can't bend again until chi is back to a quarter.
- Creative players spend no chi.

## Mastery
Each element has a mastery from 0 to 1. It grows from chi you spend and bending hits you land.

**Use all of it.** Practice is counted per ability (each key, with or without Shift), and each ability teaches less the more it has already taught. **One move used alone tops out at about 0.3.** Reaching 0.95 takes about nine abilities used well. Repeating one move over and over stops teaching you.

**Who you fight matters:**
- a plain mob, animal or non-bender teaches half as much;
- bender NPCs teach 1× (novice), 2× (adept) or 3× (master);
- other players who bend teach 1× to 3×, by their mastery;
- blocking a hit, turning it back or escaping a trap counts as a landed hit.

**Where you train matters (×1.25):** fire by day, water by night, earth below Y 40, air above Y 120. Fire under the comet and water under a full moon get ×2.

**What mastery scales (novice → master):** force in big moves ×0.85 → ×1.4, plain strikes ×0.95 → ×1.1, guard strength ×0.75 → ×1.5, most blocks an earth rip tears out 36 → 72, flame size ×0.85 → ×1.15, most water held from 69 blocks up to the server's maximum. Move costs drop from ×1.15 to ×0.8.

There is no mastery screen for players yet. Each breakthrough to a new level gives a sound and a burst, and an operator can show the mastery card with `/avatar mastery`.

## Training
Training drills teach your **basics**, the six meanings of your keys (see [Getting started](getting-started.md#your-first-basics)). Press **N**, choose **Practice**, pick a drill and press **Begin**. Only one drill runs at a time. A hostile creature nearby pauses it; dying, logging out or walking away cancels it.

Your element's **pull** drill is open from birth. The others wait as listed.

## The four teachers
Four creatures from the show each teach a **style** to a bender of their own element. **They don't give bending to anyone**: you must already bend that element, and a non-bender learns nothing from them. In every trial, a break of more than 30 seconds wipes your progress, and you are told how it goes at a third and two thirds of the way.

| Teacher | Element | Trial | Style you learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sky bison** | air | ride your own bison at least 20 blocks above the ground for 60 seconds | **bison bond**: your bison hears your whistle from anywhere and hits anyone who hurts you with a tail gust; **Z** while flying is a tail-flick gust, and glides last longer and cost less |
| **Badgermole** | earth | calm one with music (goat horn, note block, or a jukebox within 10 blocks), then stay within 7 blocks of it while it digs for 40 seconds | **badgermole sense**: feel creatures through the ground (**Shift + Z** pulse), and the ability to bend metal |
| **Ran and Shaw** (dragons) | fire | take a Sun Warriors' Flame from the Eternal Flame (empty hand), use it on the Sun Stone, then follow their dance for 8 seconds without slipping or going more than 14 blocks away | **the dragons' fire**: your fire is as strong by night as by day, and a breathing pulse makes moves started on it cheaper and brighter |
| **The moon** (Tui and La) | water | at night with the moon showing (not a new moon), crouch still on the ground within 6 blocks of the spirit koi for 20 seconds | **the moon's blessing**: water you send out surges and draws back; you can fill Spirit Water at the pool, once a night (and get one now) |

Where to find them: see [The world](world.md#creatures).

## Masters and forms
A **form** is a move with its own shape, kept in your book. You can learn forms from masters and from other players.

**From a master NPC:** right-click a master of your own element with an empty hand. They say "Watch." and show one of their forms. Repeat it with good tries in a row: 5 for a small form, 8 for a medium one, 10 for a big one. Tries less than 8 ticks apart don't count. The lesson ends if you go more than 10 blocks away or make no try for 30 seconds.

**From a player:** they use **Teach** on a form in their J screen (they must have used it to at least 0.3 practice). You have 60 seconds to accept.

A learned copy goes into your book slightly changed, and you're asked whether to put it on its key (**[Put it on]** or **[Keep mine]**). Each form carries a lineage line, such as "Taught to you by…".

- **Dialects:** each master's forms are rolled from its region (desert, mountain, snow, jungle, coast, plains). Masters within the same 256-block area share a style.
- **Schools:** `/avatar school name <words>` names at least 2 of your own forms as a school. A student who learns 60% of its forms carries its name.

## Scrolls
**Technique scrolls** (about 80) explain how to do a technique. They unlock nothing and aren't used up. They drop from bender NPCs and turn up in many chests.

**Teaching scrolls** open a forbidden or lost art for a bender of their element, then crumble.

## The Avatar cycle
Only one player in a world can be the Avatar. The order is **water → earth → fire → air**, and round again: from the element you were born to, you learn the next three in that order.

1. Reach **Expert (0.6)** in the element you learned last.
2. Find the **master** of your next element (see [The world](world.md#structures)) and right-click them with an empty hand. If you are ready, they spar you. If not, they tell you what you lack.
3. **The spar:** only the two of you can hurt each other, and nobody dies. It ends when someone drops to 30% health. Going more than 32 blocks apart or 5 minutes passing calls it off. If the master yields, they teach you their element.

You can also ask a **player** who is an Expert in your next element: `/avatar master <player>`. They have 2 minutes to **[Accept]** or **[Decline]**, then you spar.

**The Avatar** is whoever learns all four. While there is one, nobody else is taught.

- **Waking the Avatar State:** meditate at the altar of an Avatar shrine (right-click it and stay near for 3 seconds).
- **X** enters the Avatar State for 30 seconds: twice the water and flames, a bigger earth rip, 1.5× reach, free chi, flight, and a shockwave out to 7 blocks as it starts. Blows land no harder. Then it rests 10 minutes. **X** again ends it early.
- **Near death:** a blow that would leave you under a quarter of your health does nothing, and the Avatar State takes over (if it's ready).
- **Dying:** the Avatar wakes still the Avatar. Only a death **in** the Avatar State ends it: you go back to your birth element (practice kept), and the cycle is broken for 30 game days.

**Past lives:** at an Avatar shrine's altar, the past Avatar of the element you bend (Kuruk, Kyoshi, Roku, Yangchen) comes and tells you of a move, once a game day. **Aang** comes to an Avatar who knows all four and has woken the State, and teaches **energybending**: hold your look on the ring as their spirit pushes back; held to the end, their bending is yours to take or give back. Slip, and their spirit throws you back.
