# Airbending

Air is the element of freedom. It pushes, pulls, lifts and carries, and it is how airbenders move: leaps, dives, the air scooter, flight on a glider staff. Stance: **spirit**.

Keys are the defaults; rebind them in Options > Controls > Avatar, and the in-game scroll (**J**) shows your own. *(This page is generated from the in-game move list by `tools/gen_moves.py`; edit `moves.json`, not this file.)*

**On this page:** [Airbending](#airbending) · [Spirit](#spirit)

## Airbending

### Push and pull

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left click** | push; at a wall or the ground right before you: blast off it, you are thrown back. Held, it is a stream of wind (below); a gust is air gathered with **V** and sent |
| **Left click** (hold) | a stream of wind blows from your hands where you look for as long as you hold, carrying along what it meets (**Shift**: broad). In the air, looking down, it is still the jet of puffs that holds you up |
| **V** (tap) | pull what you look at to you (at a wall or the ground, nobody near your crosshair: it pulls you there). Held, nothing goes by itself: you take hold of the air (below) |
| **V** (hold), then move your look, or walk | air gathers in your hands: the farther your hand travels, the more. **Left click** sends it: a harder push, then a gust that follows your look. Holding still gathers nothing (it draws a breath, below) |
| **V** (hold), then a hard jerk of your look | a yank: whoever or whatever you look at flies to you; nothing there and a wall on your look: you fly to the wall |
| **Shift** + **V** (hold) | draw the air in from all round you to your hands, and loose items and small creatures with it; **Left click** blows it out as breath of wind, let go and it goes quietly |
| **V** (hold), then **Left click** | breath of wind: draw a breath in, then blow it out in a cone; it throws whoever it meets, puts out fire, candles and campfires, and frosts the water it blows over |
| **V**, then **G** | air ball: the air in your hands packs into a spinning ball over your palm, drawing in the items near (the marble trick); **Left click** throws it and it goes where you look: it bounces off walls, bowls people over and picks up what it rolls over |
| **V**, **G**, then **Z** | air scooter: hop on your air ball, as Aang does |
| **Right click** (hold) | take hold of the air where you look. Sweep your look and a current of air runs the way you sweep, carrying along whatever it meets: your sweeps are your guard. Circle your look and the air winds down into a whirl there; keep circling and it stands up into a tornado that runs off where you look. Swing the air up round you from your legs: a spout. **Left click** while holding it: a beam of wind for two seconds. A tap does nothing |
| **Right click** (at someone) | a bubble of air round them: it slows them and turns shots aside while you hold it; **V** then drags it to you (a master, or one who read Zaheer's scroll, draws its air out) |
| **Right click** (hold, at a wall right before you) | a skin of wind along the wall while you hold it |
| **G** | pack the air where you look into a dense pocket (**G** again: it bursts outward) and compress your air into slices, or loosen it again |
| **G** (at someone) | a cushion of packed air stalls them (**G** again: it bursts and throws them) |

### Raise and ride

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **R** (hold), then raise your look | you take hold of the air where you look, and nothing rises until your look moves. Raised: an updraft bursts up there, under whoever stands there, and holds them as high as you look; lower your look to set them down |
| **R** (hold), then a hard jerk up | the air bursts straight up there, harder than the updraft (again, if it had risen) |
| **R** (hold), then circle your look over the spot | a whirl winds up there; keep circling and it stands up into a tornado, with no second press. Let go of **R** and it runs off where you look (a whirl soon dies down; a tornado carries what it meets and throws it out) |
| **R**, then **Left click** | slam down what the updraft threw up |
| **R**, then **Z** | ride your updraft up |
| **R** (at a whirl you just made), then **Left click** | a whirl stands into a tornado, then runs down your look and throws out what it carried |
| run a lap, then **R** | a tornado round you, Aang's against Bumi (or run the lap with **R** held): each lap more makes it taller; shots and rocks thrown into it are carried round and thrown back at whoever threw them, creatures caught in it are spun up and flung out |
| **R** (at your feet) | air scooter (**R** or **Right click** again: off) |
| **R** (in the air) | air spout (**R** again lands). On it, **Right click** works as on the ground: sweep for the current |
| **R** (in the air, with the flight trait) | flight, a trait given by command (/avatar trait): you fly freely, as Zaheer; **R** again lands |

### What you just made

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Keys on it | right after you make something and while you look at it, the next key acts on it: **Right click** held takes it in your hand (a whirl, a tornado, the Air Ball, a bubbled creature, a form; a wind skin or a pocket is steered where you look), **V** held draws it back, **R** stands it taller; a push or gust still flying: **V** calls it back, **Right click** takes it in your hand, **G** bursts it where it is |
| In your hand | air is where your hand is and carries what it meets: drag a tornado through a crowd, sweep a gust and it lays its current along your sweep; your running carries into a flick; let go moving and it flies on (a tornado runs off along the ground), still and it disperses; circle your view with it, or let go circling, and it spins up round you; the wheel: nearer / farther; **Left click**: driven off along your look; **V** held takes it on (let go of **Right click** and **V** keeps it; press **Right click** again and it is back in your hand); **V** on one thrown a moment ago: called back; **Shift**: all your air near |

### Moving

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Z** | leap (in the air: dive); at someone, on the ground: a sweep that trips them; at a wall right before you: kick off it |
| **Z** (high in the air) | air bomb: dive into the ground from high up and a dome of wind and dust throws everyone round away |
| **Shift** + **Z** | air sweep, sent as you let go: a tap is the usual fan; hold and sweep your look, and it is as wide as what you swept (narrow reaches farther, wide costs more). In the air: the cushion, a soft bed of air that stops your fall, once a jump |
| **Space** (in the air) | dash |
| Glider staff: **Right click** | glide |

## Spirit

A stance of airbending. Hold **Left Alt** and pick it on the wheel (tap **Left Alt** for the plain element again). How to unlock it is under *Taking the stance*.

### Taking the stance

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left Alt** (hold), point at Spirit | take the spirit stance (tap **Left Alt**: plain air) |

### Moves

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **G** (hold, still) | leave your body: fly your spirit with the movement keys; creatures near it glow |
| **R** (hold, still) | meditate: chi comes back three times as fast while you stay still |
| **Left click** (spirit out) | spook the creatures near it: mobs flee, players are slowed |
| **V** or **G** (spirit out) | back into your body |
| **Z** (spirit out) | at full mastery (all ten marks): your body steps to where your spirit is, up to 16 blocks, once a projection |
| Spirit sight | in this stance, invisible creatures and creatures behind walls within 12 blocks glow pale blue |
