# Controls

All of the mod's keys are in **Options > Controls > Avatar**. The **J** scroll shows every move with whatever keys you have bound.

## Mod keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **V** (hold) | bend: take hold of your element |
| **R** | shape and raise, for every element (at your feet: ride it) |
| **G** | change its state: freeze, thaw, heat |
| **Z** | your feet: stomp (hold to gather) |
| **H** | fire or let go of the cable (cable spool leggings, metalbenders) |
| **B** (hold + scroll) | the water power meter: how much water you take (1 to 10) |
| **K** | bending on or off |
| **J** | the scroll of every move |
| **Left Alt** (hold) | the stance wheel (tap: back to your plain element) |
| **X** | the Avatar State (once you are the Avatar); at someone within touch, energybending |
| **N** | the N menu: training drills and the move builder |

## Vanilla keys the mod uses
- **Left click**: strike (punch, whip, push, slice, throw what you hold).
- **Right click**: shape with your hand, trace, shield.
- **Shift**: the wide version, more matter, all round you.
- **Jump**: earth bump, flips.
- **Scroll wheel**: moves what you hold nearer or farther; with **B** held, sets power.

## The grammar
Every element uses the same keys in the same way, so what you learn for one carries over.

- **V takes hold, R raises, left click strikes, right click shapes, G changes state, Z is your feet.**
- **Shift** means wider or more.
- **Holding a key** makes the bigger version.
- **Your look is your hand.** A grip held still spreads. A slow pull lifts, a hard jerk of the mouse shoots, a sweep spreads, a circle winds. How far and how fast you move decides how big the move is.
- **Running** carries your speed into what you throw, so it goes farther. It never starts another move.
- **Plain strikes** (a punch, whip, push, slice, punched-out earth or a thrown rock) come at most twice a second: each waits half a second after the last, and the key dims on the panel. Held into its bigger move (a blast, a gust, a torrent) a strike never waits.
- **Combos**: some moves are a key then another, for example **Z then R**. A strip of wooden caps under the crosshair shows each key you pressed, and the move's name appears in gold when the combo lands.

Every move for every element is listed on the element pages: [earth](earth.md), [water](water.md), [fire](fire.md), [air](air.md).

## Guards and big moves
- **A raised shield** (fire's right click, water's or lava's painted shield, air's shield) stops punches, whips, rocks and gusts for a little chi each. Earth's walls stop what is thrown at them.
- **Big moves** break a shield and hit through it, a little softened: a strong blast, lightning, combustion, a wave or flood, a great mass of water, a rock of four blocks or more, a ram, a stomp, and air's updraft, slam, tornado and sweep.
- **Caught**: someone who is rooted (ice, earth, vines, blood, a cable), carried by a wave, in a tornado, slammed into a wall, has just lost their shield, or was let go from a choke takes **half again as much** from the next hit within three seconds. A glint over their head shows it.
- Bender NPCs guard too. Adepts and masters answer a raised shield with a big move.

## The panel (bottom left)
A small parchment panel shows your element's sigil and name, your chi bar, a gathering meter, a gauge of held water or lava, and each key with its verb. It fades after a few idle seconds. With bending off it shows **BENDING OFF** and the key to turn it back on.

## The stance wheel
Hold **Left Alt**: your plain element sits at the top with every stance you have opened around it. Point at one and let go to take it. A quick tap goes back to your plain element. If you know more than one element, they sit on an inner ring with each one's stances outside it. See [Stances](stances.md).

## The N menu and the move builder
**N** opens a menu with two choices:
- **Practice**: the training drills that teach your basics (see [Progression](progression.md#training)).
- **Editor**: your own moves, or **make a new one**. Choose what it is made of (only sources you have found), the key (only keys whose basic you know), where it comes from, then shape it on an empty grid.

In the builder: click adds a block, right click removes one, Shift + click paints. It has layers, Mirror and Undo (**Ctrl+Z** / **Ctrl+Y**). On the right you set the name, the key (any key, Shift + key, or a combo such as "Z then R"), a size slider that your mastery limits, whether it grows while held, and whether it comes up round you or where you look. **Keep** sends the move to the server, which checks it. Bigger moves need longer combos. Your moves are on the **Your moves** page of the element's J tab.

## Without bending
A non-bender (bending taken, or set to none) fights as a chi-blocker:

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left click** at a creature, empty hand | a jab: low on the legs it slows them, high on the arms it weakens them |
| three jabs, quickly | their chi is blocked: a bender bends nothing for a while and drops what they held; anyone goes limp a moment |
| **Jump** in the air | a flip, once until you land |
| **Z** | dodge the way you are going: blows and shots miss you for a moment |
| **Shift** + **Z** | a sweep at the legs of those in front: they are down a moment |
