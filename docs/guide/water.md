# Waterbending

Water is the element of change. You bend what is there: lakes, rain, plants, snow, a water skin, even damp air. Water stays joined to the lake you pull it from, freezes with **G** and melts back. Hold **B** and scroll to set how much you take. Stances: **plants**, **blood** and **healing**.

Keys are the defaults; rebind them in Options > Controls > Avatar, and the in-game scroll (**J**) shows your own. *(This page is generated from the in-game move list by `tools/gen_moves.py`; edit `moves.json`, not this file.)*

**On this page:** [Waterbending](#waterbending) · [Plants](#plants) · [Blood](#blood) · [Healing](#healing)

## Waterbending

### Draw and hold

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **V** | draw a little water (**Shift**: twice as much) |
| **V** (hold), then pull: move your look, or walk back | more water keeps coming for as long as you pull, up to what you can hold; pulled from a lake it stays joined to it. A lot of water drags your look while you pull. A hard jerk of your look snaps it off as it is, and no more comes |
| **V** (still held) + **Left click** | pull more in (hold: keep pulling) |
| **V** (held, at a lake) | the water stays joined to the lake; drag it where you look |
| **V** (after a throw, not looking at water) | call your water back (within five seconds and 24 blocks) |
| **V** again (water held) | draw it in tighter: a form back to the ring, back round you from in front; at water, more drawn in; hold: your thrown water flows back into it. It never lets go (**Z** pours it) |
| **Middle click** | draw more |
| Wheel out / in | hold it in front / back round you as the ring |
| Wheel in (at the ring) | wear it; in the vanilla look, into your water skin |
| **Shift** (held still a moment and a half) | the band: your water closes into one band circling you, apart from the ring your whip rests in; it stops shots from every side; **Left click** fires water bullets off it (hold: a volley), **Shift** + **Left click** sends it out all round as a wave, **Shift** + wheel in grows it into an octopus (swinging held water all the way round you crouched makes it too) |
| **V** (no water near) | water drawn out of the plants round you or aimed at (they wither); no plants either: a little out of the damp air (more in wet lands, none in the Nether) |
