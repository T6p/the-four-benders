# Firebending

Fire is the element of power and breath. It comes from you, not the world, so it needs no source, and it is stronger by day and under the comet. Stances: **lightning** and **combustion**.

Keys are the defaults; rebind them in Options > Controls > Avatar, and the in-game scroll (**J**) shows your own. *(This page is generated from the in-game move list by `tools/gen_moves.py`; edit `moves.json`, not this file.)*

**On this page:** [Firebending](#firebending) · [Lightning](#lightning) · [Combustion](#combustion)

## Firebending

### Stances

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left Alt** (hold) | the stance wheel: point the mouse at a stance you have found and let go; each has its own moves on the same keys, and the keys it does not use stay fire's |
| **Left Alt** (tap) | back to plain fire |

### How fire answers

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Your fire | a key acts first on what you just made (a wall, a ring, flames in your hands, a flame in flight) while you keep pressing within a second and look at it; pause or look away and keys are plain again |
| A flame in flight | your newest one: **V** calls it back to your hands, **Right click** held takes it in your hand, **G** bursts it where it is; **Left click** always sends a new one |

### Attacks

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left click** | punch (hold: it grows into a blast; a strong one breaks walls; **Shift**: a fan of three) |
| **Left click** (looking down at your feet) | fire bomb: the fist strikes the ground and fire races out all round (hold first: bigger) |
| **Shift** + **Left click** (hold, hands empty) | Breath of Fire: a short breath from the mouth that swells into Iroh's breath of fire |
| **Z** | kick (**Shift**: all round; in the air: axe kick; looking down in the air: dive) |
| **Z** (hold), then sweep your look | the kick grows as your look moves, never by waiting. Swept across, it goes out as a wave of fire along the ground over what you swept; a full turn is the ring kick |
| **Z** (at your feet) | slide kick, low along the ground |
| **Z**, **Left click** | the kick's flames still in the air go off |
| **Left click** (at a fire) | it leaps out of where it burns along your look |

## Lightning

A stance of firebending. Hold **Left Alt** and pick it on the wheel (tap **Left Alt** for the plain element again). How to unlock it is under *Taking the stance*.

### Taking the stance

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left Alt** (hold), point at Lightning | take the lightning stance (tap **Left Alt**: plain fire) |

### Moves

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left click** (hold), then follow the four stances | the bolt, gathered by a kata of your two hands; **Left click** is your right hand, **Right click** your left. The words over your hotbar name each stance in turn: right hand out, left hand joins, right hand lets go, both together. Be in each stance when its beat ends and the lightning grows by a quarter; fall out of one and it goes off on you. Let go of both hands and it flies where you look, as strong as the stances you held |
| **Right click** (hold) | redirect stance: others' flames that reach you go out along your look; a bolt is caught in your left hand: throw your right hand out (**Left click**) before it is through you and it leaves where you look; too late, it goes through you |
| **G** (at water or metal) | electrify it: it stays charged a while |
| Every other key | plain fire's |

## Combustion

A stance of firebending. Hold **Left Alt** and pick it on the wheel (tap **Left Alt** for the plain element again). How to unlock it is under *Taking the stance*.

### Taking the stance

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left Alt** (hold), point at Combustion | take the combustion stance (tap **Left Alt**: plain fire) |

### Moves

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left click** (hold) | draw a breath in; the third eye glows. Stand still and keep your look steady and the breath stays calm; walking, turning or being in the air makes it unsteady |
| **Left click** (let go) | the blast leaves the third eye where you look. A full, calm breath is the whole blast; a short or unsteady one is weaker; a breath held too long goes by itself |
| Every other key | plain fire's: in the show a combustionbender bends only the third eye's blast |
