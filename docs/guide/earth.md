# Earthbending

Earth is the element of standing your ground. What you tear loose is a real physics body (Sable): it tumbles, stacks and crushes. **R** shapes the earth where it stands, **V** takes it loose. Stances: **lava** and **metal**; sand comes with the plain element.

Keys are the defaults; rebind them in Options > Controls > Avatar, and the in-game scroll (**J**) shows your own. *(This page is generated from the in-game move list by `tools/gen_moves.py`; edit `moves.json`, not this file.)*

**On this page:** [Earthbending](#earthbending) · [Lava](#lava) · [Metal](#metal)

## Earthbending

### Your hands: **R** shapes the earth where it is, **V** takes it loose

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **R** or **V** (hold) | grip the earth you look at: the ground, a wall, a ceiling, the ground under a creature or under your own feet. Nothing moves until your look does |
| held still | the grip spreads: the longer you hold, the wider the piece (cracks show it) |
| **V**, then a pull any way | the earth you gripped tears loose into your hand as one chunk (held still first: a bigger chunk); **Left click** throws it. A ceiling comes down instead (held still first: all of it with room under it) |
| **V**, then a hard jerk of your look | the scatter: block after block tears out of the ground you gripped and flies where you are looking, each a little off (held still first: more blocks). Each one costs chi as a punched block does |
| **V** on the ground, then **Left click** (hold) | the rock barrage: the block you gripped is punched out, and while you hold **Left click** the earth round it follows, block after block, each flying where you are looking at that moment (sweep your look to spray them, keep it on one target to hammer it). Each costs chi as a punched block does. Held still first, the grip punches its whole chunk out at once instead |
| **V** at your own feet, then a pull any way | you drop into the earth and go through it where you look while **V** is held; let go and you come up |
| **Shift** + **V** at your own feet, then a pull any way (or **V** at a loose rock you stand on) | the ground under you comes loose with you on it and carries you where you look; look straight down at it and it hangs. It costs a lot of chi, and none comes back meanwhile; let go and it drops |
| **R**, then a hard jerk out of the surface | a spike shoots out, as long as the jerk went (a wide grip: a cluster of them). It takes a real jerk of the mouse: a quick pull is still a heave |
| **R**, then a pull, quick or slow | everything you loosened follows your hand as fast as you move it: a pillar, a ledge, a wall. It goes back down with your hand too, and stands where you let go. One pillar pulled all the way out and off to the side slides along the ground after your hand (stand on it and it carries you) |
| **R**, then your look into the surface | a ripple runs out through the ground along your look, tripping whoever it passes under and bumping loose rocks along (a wide grip: a wider ripple) |
| **R**, then your look along the surface | the ground slides with your hand, carrying whoever stands on it |
| **R** + **Right click** (hold), then let go of **Right click** | the same wave as **Z** held, charted more exactly; nothing goes off while **Right click** is held. Sweep your look for a fan, or tap **Left click** to drop corners (one tap: a line to it; two: a cone between them), or hold **Left click** and draw: it covers everything between you and what you drew. **G**, or a hard jerk of your look up before drawing: spikes; again, or a jerk down: pillars |
| before you draw: a hard jerk of your look up or down | up: spikes. Down (or no jerk): pillars, which come up one after another, throw up whatever stands on them and go back down. Dust on the ground shows what will come (red: spikes, yellow: pillars) |
| **R** on a wall or a ceiling, then a pull any way | its earth pushes out of it, as far as your hand travels (held still first: wider); it goes back in as your hand comes back |
| **R** on a wall or a ceiling, then a hard jerk any way | a spike shoots out of it, leaning the way you jerked (a wide grip: several) |
| **V** on a ceiling, then a pull | a piece of it drops; held still first: everything with room under it comes down |
| **R** at a creature, then a hard jerk up | a column throws it; a pull lifts it |
| **R** at your own feet, then a hard jerk up | the launch; a pull carries you up on a pillar |
| **R** at your own feet, then your look a little ahead along the ground | the earth wave: the ground under you rolls ahead and carries you where you look while **R** is held |
| **Shift** while gripping | planted: you cannot walk, but you lift more and strain less. Planted on the ground you grip, a pull raises it round you as a ring; look on up to the sky and it closes over your head, a shelter that stands while you are under it; a hard jerk: a ring of spikes (it rests a few seconds between) |
| Wheel while gripping | your hand nearer or farther. Nearer, from the surface: the earth is pulled out toward you (out of a wall too) |
| **Left click** while gripping | shoved: the loosened earth is punched out along your look; heaved earth is thrown whole |
| **Z** while gripping | pushed down: the ground sinks into a pit (its earth squeezed out round the rim), a ceiling drops, heaved earth goes back into the ground |
| Let go | earth standing on the ground stays; loose earth that is moving flies on; held still, it settles |
| The weight | a lot of earth slows your walk and takes your sprint and jump; really big pieces drag your look; more than you can handle slips and you stagger |

### Earth that stands, and rocks

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **R** (at a pillar or wall) | grip it: look up and it grows taller, look down and it lowers to any height; pull it to the side and one pillar slides along the ground after your hand (stand on it: you ride it); **Z** puts it all the way back |
| **R** + **Right click** (hold) + mouse, on a standing wall | it leans: a slant, a ramp. Past its balance it topples |
| Wheel in, gripping a wall or ring of three parts or more | its parts close in |
| **V** (at a loose rock or yours still flying; at a pillar or wall, then a pull) | into your hand (a wall: the part you look at; held still first: the whole wall) |
| **V** + **Right click** (hold) + mouse, a rock in hand | turn it |
| Wheel, a rock in hand | nearer / farther; at its closest, three more notches inward: it closes round you as armor (**V** takes it off again); or jerk your look hard down at yourself with the rock in hand |
| **Left click**, wearing earth armor (at a creature, or at no earth near) | its stone glove flies, one hand then the other. Held: it grabs what it reaches and drags it back to you; let go before it lands: a fist that punches |
| ... a hard jerk of your look while the glove holds someone | slammed the way you jerked, and pinned to the ground or wall they meet, the glove their cuff, for a few seconds |
| Wearing earth armor, in the air, walking into a wall of earth | your rock shoes hold on it: jump climbs, **Shift** goes down, sideways walks along it; no fall is counted. It costs a little chi |
| **Left click**, a rock in hand | throw it (hold: a gathered throw that craters where it lands) |
| **Shift** + **Left click**, a rock in hand | a tap: it bursts into its blocks, flung where you look. Held: block after block breaks off it and flies where you are looking, until one is left in your hand |
| **Left click** (at a pillar or wall) | the block you look at is punched out of it and flies; what it held up comes down. **Shift**: the whole wall is thrown, if you have the strength for it |
| **Left click** | punch a block out of the ground or a wall; at a creature, a block is punched at it; at a big rock, flying or not, it breaks into its parts and loses its force |
| **G** | change: a flying rock breaks, what you made crumbles, the ground under a creature or bare ground goes soft; falling, the ground below turns to sand |
| **Middle click** | merge into the terrain: your earth that moves (a rock you hold, a wall you pushed or threw, a pillar you slide; held or looked at) becomes solid ground where it is, if it touches the ground or a wall. A whole raised wall goes together. A rock held in the open air: a loose rock near your look is pulled into it instead |

### Your feet

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Z** (tap) | a light stomp: the ground jolts and a stone hops up in front of you; **Left click** shoots it, **V** takes it |
| **Z** (hold, looking out), then let go | the wave: the earth ripples out from your feet where you look, as far as you look (past your reach: as far as you reach). Sweep your look while you hold and it opens into a fan over what you swept. It is pillars, which come up one after another, throw up whoever stands on them and go back down; **G** while you hold compresses them into spikes, weaker the farther out (**G** again: pillars). Let go sends it |
| **Z** (hold, looking out), then **Right click** | the trap: two walls come up at the two ends of what you swept, as far out as you look, and slam together; whoever is between them is hurt and held until they go back down. Not swept: a wall to each side of the spot you look at. As spikes (**G**) it hurts more |
| **Z** (hold, looking out), then **Left click** | the ram: a wall comes up in front of you, as wide as what you swept, and slides out to where you look, carrying whoever is in its way and throwing them on where it stops; then it goes back down (**Middle click** first: it stays, as ground). As spikes (**G**) it hurts more |
| **Z** (hold, looking down at your feet) | a heavy stomp, bigger the longer you held; **Left click** right after: the rock slide. On sand, gravel or dry dirt it throws up a cloud of dust: whoever is in it is blinded, and mobs lose you while you hide in it |
| **Z** (at a pillar or wall) | it goes back into the ground (another bender's only if you are stronger, else it shudders); a creature beside it: the ground closes over it, the tomb |
| **Z** (standing on your own pillar) | it lowers with you |
| **Z** (in the air) | drive down |
| **Space** again in the air | a short column bumps you higher |
| **Shift** + **Z** (tap) | feel the ground (the badgermoles' lesson) |

### Under the ground

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **R** held, wheel out before you move | your grip sinks into the ground along your look. Only you see its outline; the ground over it trembles, and you hear what it is in (stone dull, a hollow empty, water, lava) |
| then **Z** | the ground over it caves in: a pit |
| then sweep | it burrows along with your hand, the surface cracking over it; look up quickly and it erupts where it is; drag it back toward you and let go: a trench |
| then a hard jerk up | a spike from deep, longer and with more round it; a pull: a bigger, deep-rooted pillar |
| then **Left click** | it bursts out of the ground |

## Lava

### Taking the stance

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left Alt** (hold), point at Lava | take the lava stance: earth's own keys, made of lava (tap **Left Alt**: plain earth) |

### Moves

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **V** (hold), a pull | the earth you grip tears loose into your hand, as earth's; at lava (or loose lava): a little comes, and more the farther you pull while **V** stays down (a hard jerk snaps it off) |
| **G** (earth held, hold) | heat it through: smoke, magma, then lava (you may let go of **V** while it heats) |
| **R** (hold), a slow pull up | the ground you grip melts and rises as a wall of lava, as high as your hand (hold still first: wider); let go, it stands a while (**Left click**: a column of it flies), then sags into a pool |
| **R** (hold), a hard jerk up | a geyser: lava bursts up out of the ground there, throwing and burning whoever stands on it (a wide grip: more vents round it) |
| **R** (hold), your hand pushed along the ground | a fissure follows your hand and runs with lava |
| **Z** (hold, looking out over the ground), let go | vents burst open one after another out to where you look, throwing and burning whoever stands there (sweep your look first: a fan of them) |
| ... **G** while you hold **Z** | magma spikes in the vents' place |
| ... **Left click** while you hold **Z** | the lava wave: a crest of lava rolls out from your feet to where you look, as wide as you swept |
| ... **Right click** while you hold **Z** | the trap: the ground under the spot smokes, glows, then melts into a pool (two seconds: time to step off it); **G** right after it melts sets it hard round whoever is still in it |
| **Z** at lava of yours that stands | it sinks back into its hole and the ground closes |
| **R** on a wall, your hand pushed in | a hole melts through it, its lava pouring out at the mouth |
| **R** on a ceiling, a pull down | lava rain: it comes down on what is under it |
| **Shift** + **R** on the ground under you, a pull up | the moat: the ground round you melts while you hold it |
| **V** on the ground, a hard jerk | lava bombs: the blocks you grip melt as they tear loose and fly where you look (**Left click** held on a grip: one after another) |
| **G** at a rock of yours in flight | it melts in the air into lava bombs |
| **G** at lava (a wall of yours, a pool) | it sets to stone as it is: magma first, then stone |
| **G** (hold) at the ground | it melts into a pool that spreads while your look rests; sweep your look and the melt goes after it as a trail |
| **G** (just after your lava hits someone) | it cools on them into a shell of stone that holds them |

## Metal

A stance of earthbending. Hold **Left Alt** and pick it on the wheel (tap **Left Alt** for the plain element again). How to unlock it is under *Taking the stance*.

### Taking the stance

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left Alt** (hold), point at Metal | take the metal stance: you need metal on you, and an empty hand (tap **Left Alt**: plain earth) |

### Moves

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Needs metal on you | an ingot, nugget or block of metal carried, metal armor worn, or the cable spool; bands sit on your forearms; the bands you shoot are iron nuggets, the whips need the cable spool worn |
| **Left click** | an iron band flies out (an iron nugget each) and clamps on where it strikes: the legs (slowed; two: held fast), the arms (they cannot strike), the eyes (blinded); it falls off after a while |
| **Shift** + **Left click** | three bands at once, fanned across your look (three nuggets) |
| **Left click** (hold) | the cable off your spool (worn), led by your hand: its tip goes where you look; swept, it cuts what it passes through; resting on a creature, it wraps it; then a hard jerk of your look up or down yanks it to you, sideways flings it that way |
| **V** (at a creature wearing your bands) | take hold of it by your bands on it: one tugs it to you, two drag it where you look, three or more lift it; a hard jerk of your look (two or more): slammed that way; **Left click**: throw it (harder the more bands); **G** with three or more: they close on it; **V** again: let go |
| **V** (hold) at a metal block, a pull | it tears out into your hand (a hard jerk: it is torn out and flung where you look); a door swings |
| **V** at metal in flight | a band, a blade, an arrow: caught out of the air |
| **V** at your bands lying about | the nuggets fly back to you |
| **Right click** | a cable (the spool reaches farther than the bands): a tap reels you in to where it struck, or the creature to you |
| **Right click** (hold) | hang on the cable and swing; let go of **Right click** and you let go of it |
| **R** (hold) | a shield of iron plates before you that goes with your look (a fast sweep spreads it wider); **Left click**: its plates fly off as blades |
| **R** (hold) at metal | iron bars: bent apart, and on as your look sweeps over them; a metal wall: torn open; a metal floor you stand on: your look pushed along it sends a ripple of metal out |
| **Shift** + **R** | plates over your body as armor (again: off) |
| **G** (metal held) | it changes form: a blade, a sheet, a lump again (**Shift**: torn into shrapnel along your look) |
| **Z** | cables stab the ground and throw you up and along your look (you land unhurt) |
| **H** | with the cable spool on: fire the cable, as **Right click** does |
