# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): while the
version starts with 0, any release may change controls, configs or saved data.

## 0.1.0 (first public release)

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.228+. Requires Sable 2.0.6+, GeckoLib 4.8+ and playerAnimator 2.0.4+.

### Added
- Earthbending, waterbending, firebending and airbending, all on one set of keys: V takes hold, R raises,
  left click strikes, right click shapes, G changes state, Z is the feet, Shift is wider or more.
- Bending by motion: a grip held still spreads, a pull lifts, a hard jerk shoots, a sweep spreads, a
  circle winds. Size comes from how your hands move, not from waiting.
- Earth as Sable physics bodies: chunks torn loose and thrown, pillars and walls to the height of the hand,
  spikes, burrows and pits, the earth wave, stone armor, gloves and cuffs, rock barrages, wall-walking,
  sand that breaks a fall.
- Water from lakes, rain, plants and the water skin: joined streams, whips, the ring, curtains, waves,
  floods, geysers, whirlpools, ice walls, spikes and domes, freezing a lake along your look, the spout,
  running on water, a power meter (B) for how much you take.
- Fire: growing punches, flame streams and whips, shields drawn with a circle, walls and rings, jets,
  heat that melts, boils and smelts.
- Air: pushes, pulls and yanks, currents along your sweep, updrafts, tornadoes from circling, the air ball
  and scooter, the spout, leaps, dives, dashes, the air bomb.
- Stances on a wheel (Left Alt): lava, metal, plants, blood, healing, lightning, combustion, spirit.
- Chi, hidden mastery that grows with varied practice, practice drills, chi-blocking for non-benders.
- Bender NPCs (novice, adept, master) with village fighting customs; masters who teach and spar.
- Sky bison, badgermoles, sun dragons and spirit koi; temples, forts, outposts, ruins, dens and shrines.
- Day, night, full moon, comet and eclipses change each element's strength.
- The Avatar cycle: one Avatar per world, the Avatar State, past Avatars at the shrines, energybending.
- J opens a scroll with every move under your own key bindings; K turns bending on and off.
- 32 advancements, configurable numbers for every move (`config/avatar-common.toml`).
